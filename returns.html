Return and Refund Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

Custom products

Personalized and custom-made products are generally final sale for changes of mind, incorrect customer-selected sizes, customer-submitted errors or approved design choices.

Defects and incorrect orders

If your item is defective or differs from your confirmed order due to our error, contact us within seven calendar days of receipt with your order number, description and photos where applicable. We will work with you on a correction, replacement or refund.

Non-custom products

Eligible unused, undamaged non-custom items in original condition may be returned within 14 calendar days of receipt. Contact us first. Customers pay return shipping unless the return results from our error.

Cancellations and refunds

Orders may generally be canceled for a full refund before materials are committed or production begins. Later requests are evaluated individually; refunds may be reduced by reasonable nonrecoverable costs. Approved refunds go to the original payment method where possible; processing times vary.

Product variations

Minor variations in color, placement, texture and appearance are not necessarily defects. Contact us if something is wrong. Nothing in this policy limits applicable consumer rights.
