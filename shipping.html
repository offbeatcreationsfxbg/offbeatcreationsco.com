Shipping and Local Pickup Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

Processing

Most custom orders are made in 5–7 business days; timing varies with complexity, materials, approval and volume. Shipping time is additional. Express service is available by request only. We will notify customers of significant delays and offer cancellation and refund options as required by law.

Shipping

Shipping options and charges appear in your estimate. Shipping availability is confirmed with your estimate. Delivery estimates are not guarantees. Tracking is provided when available. Customers must provide accurate addresses; additional charges may apply to reshipment due to customer address errors.

Lost or damaged shipments

Contact us promptly, retain damaged goods and packaging, and provide photos when possible. We will work with you and the carrier toward an appropriate resolution.

Local pickup

Local pickup is by arrangement in the Fredericksburg area. We notify you when your order is ready and arrange a time and location. We do not operate a public walk-in storefront. Contact us if you cannot collect your order.

Notifications

We may email updates about order confirmation, payment, production, shipment and pickup.
